Kinnred Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20-07-2026 · Last updated: 20-07-2026

Madtechsolutions operates the Kinnred mobile application, the website kinnred.com. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the rights you have over it. It applies to everyone who visits our website, joins our waitlist, or creates a Kinnred account.

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & profile information — name, date of birth, phone number, email address, gender identity (“I identify as”), the genders you want to be shown (“show me”), your “here for” intent tags (dating, friends, activities, networking, casual chats), profile photos (up to 5), bio, and interest tags.
  • Verification data — a one-time password (OTP) sent to your phone number, and a live selfie captured for identity/liveness verification. We use the selfie to confirm you are a real person matching your profile photos; we do not use it to build a biometric template for any purpose beyond that verification and fraud-prevention check.
  • Location data — your device’s location, used to show you people and Circles nearby and to calculate distance. We only ever display an approximate distance to other users (e.g., “0.4 km away”) — never your precise coordinates or exact address.
  • Content you create — Pings and chat messages, Circle posts and comments, Circles you create or join, and reports you file against other users.
  • Verification & safety signals — your verified badge status, block/report history (yours and against you), and moderation outcomes, kept so we can enforce our Community Guidelines consistently.
  • Optional data — an emergency contact’s name and number, only if you choose to use the in-app check-in feature before an in-person meetup.
  • Usage & device data — app interactions (grid views, pings sent/accepted, circles joined), device model, operating system, IP address, and crash/diagnostic logs, collected automatically to keep the Service reliable and secure.
  • Payment data (Premium) — if you subscribe to Kinnred Premium, our payment processor handles your card/UPI details directly; we receive only a subscription status and transaction reference, never your full payment credentials.

We do not collect data from your device contacts, and we never ask for your exact GPS coordinates to be shared with other users.

2. Why We Collect It

We process your personal data to:

  • create and verify your account, and keep the Grid free of fake or duplicate profiles;
  • show you relevant people and Circles nearby, and show them you;
  • operate the photo-lock safety mechanic described in Section 3;
  • deliver Pings, chat, and Circle features, including push notifications;
  • detect, investigate, and act on fraud, harassment, spam, or safety reports;
  • provide customer support and respond to your requests;
  • improve the Service (aggregated, de-identified analytics only — never sold);
  • comply with legal obligations, including the DPDP Act and requests from law enforcement or the Data Protection Board of India; and
  • process payments and manage Premium subscriptions.

We rely on your consent for location access, camera/photo access, and push notifications, each of which you can withdraw at any time in your device or app settings (see Section 8). Where we act to keep the platform safe (e.g., reviewing a report or a suspected fake account), we do so on the basis of our legitimate interest in protecting all users, which we’ve weighed against your privacy interests.

3. How the Photo-Lock Safety Mechanic Works

By default, women’s profile photos are locked (blurred) and are only visible to a specific person the instant that person’s Ping is accepted by that user. This is a data-visibility control we enforce at the server level, not just in the app’s interface — a locked photo is never sent to a device that hasn’t been granted access. Verified users may be offered expanded visibility as an additional trust feature; any such setting is opt-in and can be changed at any time in MySpace → Settings. All users can additionally choose to lock their own photos regardless of gender.

4. Who We Share Data With

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who process data on our behalf under contract, including cloud hosting and storage (AWS, Mumbai region), CDN (Cloudflare), OTP delivery (MSG91/Twilio), push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging), and automated content-moderation screening (e.g., AWS Rekognition/Sightengine) for photos, backed by human review;
  • Other users, limited to what your profile and privacy settings make visible to them (Section 3 governs photos specifically);
  • Circle admins, limited to the information a Circle’s tools expose (e.g., your display name, profile photo per your lock settings, and RSVP status) — never your phone number;
  • Law enforcement or regulators, only where required by Indian law, a valid legal process, or to protect the safety of a user or the public; and
  • A successor entity, if Kinnred is ever involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale — you’ll be notified before your data is transferred and becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

Some service providers may process data outside India as part of their global infrastructure. Where this happens, we require contractual safeguards consistent with the DPDP Act’s cross-border transfer requirements, and we keep primary storage of Indian users’ data in the Mumbai AWS region.

5. Data Retention

We keep personal data only as long as it serves the purpose it was collected for:

  • Profile data — for as long as your account is active, plus a limited window after deletion to prevent abuse (e.g., a banned user re-registering).
  • Chats & Pings — retained while your account is active so you can access your conversation history; deleted on account deletion, subject to the safety-investigation exception below.
  • Verification selfie — retained only for as long as needed to confirm your identity and resolve any fraud dispute, then deleted.
  • Reports & moderation records — retained longer than the underlying content, so we can identify repeat offenders and respond to legal requests.
  • Account deletion — when you delete your account, we erase or irreversibly anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except where we’re legally required to retain it (e.g., an open safety investigation or a statutory record-keeping obligation).

6. Data Security

We use encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access controls, and automated + human content moderation to protect your data. No system is 100% secure. If a breach occurs that risks your rights, we will notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected users in line with DPDP Rules timelines, and take immediate steps to contain and remediate it.

7. Children’s Privacy

Kinnred is restricted to users 18 and older, verified at onboarding. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn an account belongs to a minor, we will suspend it and delete the associated data.

8. Your Rights

Under the DPDP Act, 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it’s processed;
  • Correct or update inaccurate or incomplete data directly in MySpace, or by request;
  • Erase your data by deleting your account, subject to Section 5;
  • Withdraw consent for optional processing (location, notifications, camera) at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal;
  • Nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity; and
  • Grieve — file a complaint with our Grievance Officer (below), and if unresolved, escalate to the Data Protection Board of India.

To exercise any of these rights, email heykinnred@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 30 days.

9. Cookies & Similar Technologies

Our website uses only essential cookies to run the waitlist form and, where enabled, privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate traffic. We do not use third-party advertising trackers. You can control cookies through your browser settings.
