Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 22, 2026

Welcome to Kinnred. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. Kinnred is a location-based social discovery platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and secure your data when you visit our landing page or use our future applications.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide a secure and customized experience. This includes:

  • Account Information: When you join our waitlist or create a profile, you provide your name, email address, city, gender, and social preferences.
  • Location Data: Kinnred is built on discovering people nearby. We collect approximate location details to place you in the local grid. We do not display exact GPS coordinates to other users.
  • Verification Data: For safety, we utilize selfie liveness verification. These verification scans are processed securely and deleted immediately once verification status is confirmed.
  • Images: Photos you upload for your profile. Your photo-locking settings dictate who can view these photos.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Create and maintain your founding waitlist profile.
  • Enable our proximity grid features, allowing neighboring users to discover your card based on your selected interests.
  • Ensure platform safety, verify user authenticity, and reduce catfishing or automated bots.
  • Send launch announcements, early access invites, and updates about platform availability in your city.

3. Photo Privacy and Lock Controls

Kinnred provides a unique privacy mechanic: **Optional Photo Locking**. If you enable Photo Lock in your privacy settings:

  • Your profile card details (name, age, distance, bio, and interests) remain visible to help others discover you.
  • Your photos are blurred by default on the local grid.
  • Your photos will unlock automatically the moment you accept their chat request or ping. You retain full control over who sees your unblurred image.

4. Data Sharing and Security

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third parties. Your location is only exposed as an approximate distance radius (e.g. "600m away") to other verified users on the grid.

We employ premium security measures to protect your database entries. However, no transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we encourage users to practice safe sharing habits.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@kinnred.com.
