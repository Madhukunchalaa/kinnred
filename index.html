Grid over swipe

Swiping is a slot machine. Kinnred is a room full of people.

An algorithm decides who you see, matches go silent, and it's dating-only even when you just wanted to make friends in a new city. So we threw the whole model out.

Swipe apps

  • One profile at a time
  • An algorithm decides who you see
  • You must match before you can message
  • Dating only
  • "Matched with 50, talked to 2"

Kinnred

  • Real people nearby, in a grid
  • You browse freely — no algorithm
  • Tap → view → decide for yourself
  • Dating + friends + activities + networking
  • One icebreaker to start — no ghost-piles

Circles are localized, topic-specific communities built right into your neighborhood. They replace disjointed WhatsApp groups and Facebook events with unified spaces to meet real people nearby who share your exact interests.

The real product

Optional photo locking,
not profile locks.

Everyone sees every profile card — name, age, distance, bio, and interests. Users can toggle photo lock mode in privacy settings. Locked photos stay blurred and unlock automatically the moment you accept their chat request.

  • No "reveal" step to learn — the unlock is the accept
  • OTP phone + selfie liveness verification
  • Approximate distance only — never exact GPS
  • Invisible browsing, instant block & report
