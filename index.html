Grid over swipe

Swiping is a slot machine.
Kinnred is a room full of people.

An algorithm decides who you see, matches go silent, and it's dating-only even when you just wanted to make friends in a new city. So we threw the whole model out.

Swipe apps

  • ✕One profile at a time
  • ✕An algorithm decides who you see
  • ✕You must match before you can message
  • ✕Dating only
  • ✕"Matched with 50, talked to 2"

Kinnred

  • ✓30–50 real people nearby, in a grid
  • ✓You browse freely — no algorithm
  • ✓Tap → view → decide for yourself
  • ✓Dating + friends + activities + networking
  • ✓One icebreaker to start — no ghost-piles

The real product

Photos locked,
not profiles.

Everyone sees every profile card — name, age, distance, bio, interests. Women's photos stay blurred by default. They unlock automatically, and only for one person, the moment she accepts that person's request.

  • No "reveal" step to learn — the unlock is the accept
  • OTP phone + selfie liveness verification
  • Approximate distance only — never exact GPS
  • Invisible browsing, instant block & report
